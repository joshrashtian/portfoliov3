# Review
> [!note] Trigger Warning; Mentions of Suicide


Persona 3 is one of the most flawed games I’ve played in a long time. The combat is either frustrating or too easy, its dungeon design on a gameplay level kind of sucks, and the game is overall extremely repetitive.

So then why did I put over 150 hours into it? Why did I give it such a basically perfect score? Why do I recommend the game to every person I see?

Persona 3 is my favorite RPG of all time. It isn’t the best, the flashiest, most immersive, but it’s my favorite because of the impact it’s left on my life and outlook on it. I ended up being a better friend to my friends as a result, ended up going to the gym constantly, started to realize I shouldn’t fear change or the future, and embrace all of life’s wonder. And that was a message I needed to hear when I played the game. 

Persona 3 is not a game that everyone will like. Hell, originally I didn’t even know if I would. It’s long, repetitive and flawed. But it’s in those imperfections I found a game that resonated so strongly with me, especially at a time I felt like I was at my lowest point. It was a game that restored myself when I lost my grip on reality, and its message only became even more important as the year went on. 

Shortly after finishing this game, someone I knew from high school commuted suicide. Recently, a family friend also passed away suddenly. And the themes of death and despair that this game ultimately doesn't shy away from at all is what makes me find comfort in it. It doesn't tell you you can change the fact your gonna die, rather you can change the way you react to it, and use that as the ultimate motivator.

But, I also found the ultimate message of rebirth the game also brings that you can change, you can become a better person and should always never stop caring, and in a time where a lot of media almost preaches that things don't matter, it is a game that ultimately teaches you the opposite; you will die and thats why you need to cherish and appreciate the little things.
